There's nothing like your own place, right?

We all have that place where we have a sense of belonging. A place where we can let our guard down and be ourselves and not worry about what anyone else has to say. It could be your bedroom, it could be a small corner of your mind, or a place in your imagination.

For Fig, that spot's the Ninth Room.

The Ninth Room's as Fig's as this space can be, and Fig's just no ordinary cat. It is filled with his things, bits and pieces, memories of him, and whatever he think is right in the Ninth Room.

Isn't it a little too tidy and factlessness? It's not really, and not every detail has an explanation. But this is the charm of your place, isn't it? It doesn't have to be clear to anyone else.

In Fig's Room He doesn't have to put on a show for anyone. Be anyone he wants. Curious Stubborn, Dramatic, Rude or just plain Fig.

People can immerse themselves in his world, discover oddities, and get to know the strange feline who lives there.

But remember just one thing.

You're a guest in Fig's world.

And believe me, Fig's got nothing against throwing it in your face.
